## I build systems that have to work after the applause.

I am **Freeman Paul Ipumbu**, a Namibian Technical Director, founder, systems builder and design science researcher based in Windhoek.

My career began close to the machinery: telecom network operations, offshore ICT, enterprise systems, security, service continuity and the records people reach for when something fails at 02:00. I now carry that operating discipline into financial technology, product architecture, full-stack delivery and digital experiences built to survive contact with the real world.

This profile is the public evidence trail of that work. The production source, credentials, personal data and protected operating material stay where they belong.


## Current orbit

- **Technical Director, TPTS Namibia.** Leading technical direction for practical, secure financial technology and the systems around it. TPTS owns its products; SolarSpin contributes selected digital-experience and engineering work where stated.

## Engineering lens

### Infrastructure before interface

The interface is only the visible edge. I care about identity, access, backups, observability, network reality, deployment, failure modes and the operator who inherits the system.

### Evidence before theatre

Public demos are labelled as demos. Controlled releases are labelled as controlled. Integration gates remain visible. A persuasive experience should never require an untrue claim.

### Privacy by boundary

Private source, credentials, client records, payment proofs, exact locations and protected operational data do not become portfolio material. Public case studies explain decisions without exposing what does not belong in public.

### Handover is part of the build

Documentation, verification, owner training and continuity are not the leftovers after launch. They are part of the product.
